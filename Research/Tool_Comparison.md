\# Phishing Simulation Tool Comparison



\## 1. Objective



Three phishing simulation/security-testing tools were considered for the controlled laboratory simulation:



\- GoPhish

\- King Phisher

\- Social Engineer Toolkit (SET)



The comparison focuses on suitability for this project rather than general tool capability.



\---



\## 2. Comparison



| Criteria | GoPhish | King Phisher | SET |

|---|---|---|---|

| Primary purpose | Phishing simulations and awareness testing | Phishing/security awareness campaigns | Social-engineering security testing |

| Campaign management | Strong | Strong | Limited compared with dedicated simulation platforms |

| Email campaign support | Yes | Yes | Yes |

| Landing pages | Yes | Yes | Yes |

| Tracking/metrics | Strong | Strong | More limited for this project's requirements |

| Reporting | Strong | Strong | More limited |

| Ease of use | High | Moderate | Moderate |

| Suitable for controlled awareness testing | Excellent | Good | Good |

| Best fit for this project | High | Medium | Medium |



\---



\## 3. GoPhish



\### Advantages



\- Designed specifically for phishing-awareness simulations.

\- Provides campaign management.

\- Supports email templates and landing pages.

\- Provides measurable campaign results.

\- Suitable for controlled laboratory testing.

\- Results can be used for quantitative analysis.



\### Limitations



\- Requires configuration of the email delivery environment.

\- Must be operated only in an authorized and controlled environment.

\- Does not replace enterprise email-security or SIEM platforms.



\---



\## 4. King Phisher



\### Advantages



\- Designed for authorized phishing simulations.

\- Supports campaign management and tracking.

\- Provides useful phishing-awareness testing capabilities.



\### Limitations



\- Deployment and configuration can be more involved.

\- Less suitable than GoPhish for our specific requirement of a simple, measurable laboratory experiment.



\---



\## 5. Social Engineer Toolkit (SET)



\### Advantages



\- Broad social-engineering testing capabilities.

\- Useful for security assessments and demonstrations.

\- Available in common penetration-testing environments.



\### Limitations



\- Broader security-testing focus rather than being primarily an awareness-campaign management platform.

\- Less suitable for the structured campaign metrics required by this project.



\---



\## 6. Tool Selection



\### Selected Tool: GoPhish



GoPhish is selected for the practical implementation because it most closely matches the project's requirements:



\- Controlled phishing campaign

\- Safe landing pages

\- Test accounts

\- Campaign tracking

\- Quantitative metrics

\- Reporting

\- Evidence collection



King Phisher and SET will remain evaluated alternatives rather than implemented tools.



\---



\## 7. Safety Considerations



The selected tool will only be used in an authorized laboratory environment.



The practical implementation will use:



\- Test accounts

\- Dummy credentials

\- Non-sensitive information

\- Isolated systems

\- Controlled recipients



Real passwords, personal information, or unauthorized external targets will not be used.



\---



\## 8. Conclusion



GoPhish provides the best balance between ease of deployment, campaign management, measurable results, and suitability for the controlled phishing-awareness experiment required by this project.



The tool selection will be validated further during practical deployment. Any deployment challenges or limitations encountered will be documented in the Project Journal.
