## Source Classification

### Primary Evidence

The SEC EDGAR Form 8-K filing is treated as the primary evidence source for the incident.

### Secondary Evidence

The Register, Security Affairs, and Cybernews are used as secondary sources to corroborate and contextualize the incident.

### Source-Derived Facts

Information directly supported by the SEC filing or corroborated by the secondary sources will be presented as incident facts.

### Analytical Assessment

Interpretations concerning attack characteristics, security weaknesses, potential consequences, and lessons learned will be clearly distinguished from directly reported facts.

### Recommendations

Security controls and future-prevention measures proposed in this project are recommendations based on the lessons identified from the incident and the controlled laboratory simulation.

---

## Important Evidence Limitation

Available reporting establishes unauthorized access to the affected mailbox and the presence of sensitive information within it.

The available reporting does not establish confirmed exfiltration of the sensitive information.

The report therefore distinguishes between:

- Confirmed unauthorized access
- Confirmed exposure/accessibility of sensitive information
- Potential downstream risks
- Unconfirmed data exfiltration

No claim of confirmed data theft will be made unless directly supported by an authoritative source.
